# Changelog

## 0.1.0 - Beta
First public beta. The full narcotics investigation is in and playable end to end.

- A narcotics case can take over the slot a murder would have used, controlled by a Hijack Chance slider (from never to always).
- A living supply chain: a cook works a shift in a hidden lab producing a uniquely named drug, a courier runs each dose to a searchable, camera-covered dead drop, and a dealer collects and sells hand to hand to buyers.
- Buyers overdose and are arrested, opening the case on your scanner; the chain is then worked with fingerprints, CCTV, tailing and interrogation back up to the lab and the cook.
- Custom case resolution at City Hall: name the producer and confirm their arrest, the lab location, the courier and the dealer, with the arrest and location graded live as you fill the form in.
- Procedurally named drugs, with the physical dose drawn from a rotating set of medical items for variety; the examine card shows the drug name and a neutral description.
- Drug doses are searchable, fingerprintable physical evidence, and cannot be taken or used by the player.
- Dead drops are chosen on well-framed CCTV so exchanges can be caught on camera; couriers and dealers face the bin while using it.
- Cadence modelled on the base game: a cook shift begins on a twelve-hour grid and the clock starts when the cook reaches the lab, so the whole shift is spent there.
- Extensive configuration for cadence and pacing, applied live.
